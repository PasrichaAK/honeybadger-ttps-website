WHO WE ARE

Technology that supports
operational readiness.

Honey Badger TTPS Private Limited is focused on providing technology, equipment and technical support for defence and other demanding operational applications, with a focus on supporting Indian Air Force and wider defence-sector requirements.

Our areas of work include navigation aids, wireless communication systems, drones and associated communication technologies. We aim to support customers through dependable sourcing, system integration, installation, commissioning, repair and maintenance services.
